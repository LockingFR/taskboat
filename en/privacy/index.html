Legal notice and privacy

Who publishes Taskboat, who hosts it and what happens to your data.

Publisher

Taskboat (taskboat.app) is an independent project published on a non-professional basis by a private individual, who remains anonymous as allowed by French law no. 2004-575 of 21 June 2004. Their identity has been provided to the host.

Contact: open a message on GitHub. Messages are public: do not include tasks, exports or personal information.

Host

Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Phone: +1 650 319 8930. cloudflare.com

Your tasks

  • Your lists go straight from Microsoft to your browser. Taskboat has no server that receives them, and the publisher cannot see them.
  • Exported files are created on your device and are not encrypted. The JSON backup contains the address of the signed-in account.
  • Taskboat changes nothing in your account.

Microsoft sign-in

You sign in on Microsoft's page. Taskboat requests Tasks.Read (read your tasks) and User.Read (read your basic profile, including the account address). Microsoft also lists maintaining access, which is part of its sign-in system. The token stays in the tab and is removed when you close it.

To remove the permission: personal account or work or school account. See Microsoft's privacy statement.

Cookies and technical data

  • Taskboat uses no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • Your language choice is stored in your browser. You can clear it with the site data.
  • Cloudflare processes the technical data needed to deliver and protect the site, including your IP address, possibly outside the European Union, under its privacy policy.
