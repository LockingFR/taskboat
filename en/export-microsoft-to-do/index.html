How to export Microsoft To Do before it retires

Microsoft retires the To Do apps on June 30, 2027, with no replacement for personal accounts and no export button in the app. Here's how to save every list in a few minutes, for free.

Export my lists with Taskboat

Export in 3 steps

  1. Open Taskboat and click "Sign in and load my lists". Sign-in happens on Microsoft's official page; Taskboat only asks to read your tasks.
  2. Check the preview: every list, its tasks, steps, notes and due dates.
  3. Download your new app's format, and keep the JSON backup too: it holds everything.

Taskboat reads your tasks straight from Microsoft inside your browser. Nothing goes through our servers and nothing in your account is changed.

Which format?

  • Todoist: Todoist CSV, one file per list, imported with "Import from CSV template" (get Todoist).
  • Obsidian, Logseq, Joplin: Markdown with checkboxes.
  • Excel, Google Sheets, Notion: CSV, one row per task.
  • Thunderbird, Nextcloud: ICS (VTODO tasks).
  • TickTick: its built-in importer works (Settings › Integrations & Import).
